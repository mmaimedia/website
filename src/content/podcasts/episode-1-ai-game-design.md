# Episode 1: AI in Game Design

In our first episode, we sit down with Dr. Sarah Chen to discuss the role of artificial intelligence in modern game development.

## Topics Covered
- Procedural content generation
- AI-driven narrative systems
- Machine learning in game testing
- The future of AI-assisted game design

## Guest
Dr. Sarah Chen is a professor of Game AI at the University of California, Santa Cruz, and has worked on AI systems for several AAA titles.
