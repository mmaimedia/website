# Nash Equilibrium Explained

In this video, we explore the concept of Nash equilibrium and how it applies to game design.

## Key Takeaways
- A Nash equilibrium occurs when no player can improve their outcome by unilaterally changing their strategy
- Every finite game has at least one Nash equilibrium
- Game designers can use this concept to predict player behavior
