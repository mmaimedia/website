# The Prisoner's Dilemma: A Game Designer's Guide

The prisoner's dilemma is one of the most famous concepts in game theory. It describes a scenario where two rational actors must decide whether to cooperate or betray each other, and the outcome depends on what the other player chooses.

## The Setup

Two players are arrested and interrogated separately. Each has two options:
- **Cooperate** (stay silent)
- **Defect** (betray the other)

The payoffs are:
- If both cooperate: both get a moderate sentence
- If one defects and the other cooperates: defector goes free, cooperator gets a harsh sentence
- If both defect: both get a harsh (but not maximum) sentence

## Application in Game Design

In game design, the prisoner's dilemma appears in:
- **Multiplayer trust mechanics**
- **Resource sharing systems**
- **Alliance and betrayal dynamics**
- **Economic simulation games**

Understanding this concept helps designers create meaningful choices that drive player engagement.
